Case study / QR food ordering platform

QR Food System

A QR ordering system for canteen menus, payment and pickup queues.

ClientKantin Sekolah Kristen Penabur
StackPHP · CodeIgniter · MySQL
StatusProfessional project
RoleFull-stack

01 / The problem

Long queues, slow orders.

Manual ordering delayed customers and made canteen workflows harder to manage.

02 / What I built

Scan, order, pick up.

A QR-based ordering flow with menu selection, QR payment, automatic queue numbers and order status updates.

03 / The result

Faster canteen service.

Customers order digitally while staff manage queues and reports in one system.

Project Overview

FMS (Fleet Management System) is a comprehensive solution designed to streamline fleet operations and enhance vehicle management efficiency. The system provides real-time tracking, maintenance scheduling, and performance analytics to support informed decision-making. FMS aims to optimize fleet utilization, reduce operational costs, and improve overall productivity for organizations managing a fleet of vehicles. With features such as route optimization, fuel monitoring, and driver behavior analysis, FMS empowers businesses to achieve greater efficiency and sustainability in their fleet operations.

Application Objectives

  • Streamline fleet operations through real-time tracking and management.
  • Optimize vehicle utilization and reduce operational costs.
  • Enhance maintenance scheduling and performance analytics for informed decision-making.
  • Improve driver behavior and safety through monitoring and analysis.
  • Checking inspection and maintenance records

System Workflow

  1. Monitor vehicle status and location in real-time.
  2. Schedule and track maintenance activities for each vehicle.
  3. Do checking inspection and maintenance records
  4. Generate performance reports and analytics.
